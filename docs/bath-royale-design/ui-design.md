## Shared design language

- Mobile-first, playful, readable and calm; retain existing Material 3 dark/light color schemes, original Portuguese (Portugal) humour and category descriptions. No full rebrand. Reduce heavy outlined fields, repetitive icons, nested cards and large headings. Use spacing, type hierarchy and subtle dividers instead. Use theme tokens, not hardcoded dark colors.
- Main navigation remains Explore / Collection / Rankings / Profile. Give each destination its own header instead of repeating Bath Royale. Move theme switching to a real Settings screen accessible from Profile. Persist theme using existing state. Show add-bathroom FAB only in Explore.
- Consistent favourite heart affordance everywhere; reserve stars for ratings. Preserve favourite state. Use accessible labels and minimum 48dp tap targets, safe areas, keyboard/large text support, sensible focus, visible errors. Keep desktop width constraints and adaptive layouts. Maintain navigation state on switching tabs. Avoid fake stats, arbitrary assigned icons/photos or fabricated community data.
- Numeric notation in examples is Portuguese (decimal comma); keep precise numeric values internally. No rating means no rating, never zero. All sample values in the reference are illustrative.

## Session 1 — Shell, Explore and bathroom details

### Explore
- Header: Explorar; compact search, sort and one filters entry point. Keep every existing filter and sort option, grouping mutually exclusive choices correctly. Remove permanently duplicated filter chip strip. Show only active removable chips and an active count on the filter button; retain a clear/reset action and meaningful empty state. Consider visited/discovered filters as derived from existing visits, not review status. Search and sorting remain independent. No functional loss.
- Compact bathroom rows/cards: name, community rating with star and count, secondary explicit label for latest personal review when present; discovered status derived from actual visits, independent of review. Optional small tag preview only if its source is clearly labelled (personal latest tags are not community consensus). Keep favourite heart tappable independently of opening details. Move full category breakdown to detail screen. Do not display personal emoji or stars as if they represent community scores. Avoid broad green/yellow/red card backgrounds unless there is a compelling accessible use. No fake data.
- For current local prototype, community = all locally available reviews, including own; future public scope is a product policy, not implemented here. Unrated, unvisited and visited-unrated are distinct.

### Bathroom details
- Header with back, bathroom name, favourite heart. Prominent rating and clear actions `Estive aqui!` / `Avaliar`. Three independently scrollable views via tabs: `Resumo`, `Avaliações`, `Visitas` (personal). Keep actions easily accessible and avoid long shared header that prevents fast access to tabs. Use lazy list builders for potentially long histories; retain sorting and all edit/delete actions.
- Summary defaults to community scope. Use arithmetic means of all relevant individual reviews (including own, once); category averages are displayed numerically with actual denominators (overall 5, hygiene/comfort/aesthetics 4, paper 2), with small bars if helpful; never round a mean into a categorical description. Community rating and count refer to same scope. Personal scope shows latest reviewed experience by visit chronology, with original categorical emoji descriptions and date. No blended average. Clear empty state independently per scope. All future published catalogue entries are intended to be public; personal history privacy is independent. Do not implement a visibility model now. Use `Comunidade` / `A tua experiência` compact switch within Summary; no extra repeated community/personal hero in other tabs. Preserve community review list, owner editing, personal visit list, confirmed visit deletion (with review warning), safe undo, and direct review of existing visit.
- On register visit: save immediately, then show a compact celebratory bottom sheet. Title `Mais um trono!` / `Visita registada!`, prompt `Como foi a experiência?`, prominent `Avaliar experiência`, secondary `Agora não`; distinct top-corner labelled undo action (undo icon + Desfazer; separate from dismissal). A close/swipe/back dismisses only the invitation, not the visit. Undo removes only the exact new unreviewed visit using existing safe state semantics. No pending-review status, reminders or counters. Reuse rapid-tap protection; guard against duplicate invitations as needed. Existing favourites and unrelated visits remain unchanged.

## Session 2 — Review wizard

- Three steps: `Global`, `Detalhes`, `Etiquetas`; lightweight step/progress indicator, back/continue, stable bottom action safe area, scroll only within step as necessary. Maintain one draft across steps; cancel/back out must not create visit or review; optionally confirm discarding edited draft. When editing, preserve exact rating, existing tags and immutable visit identity; on save perform existing atomic storage operation exactly once. Preserve validation, including explicitly selected 0 versus unanswered and existing tag length/count constraints. Focus first invalid input; no invisible validation failures.
- Step 1 (updated in Session 4A): compact fixed context row with bathroom name and `Acabei de visitar` or the exact visit date. No Alterar action or visit-selection sheet. Direct review creates a new visit on successful save; explicit history/invitation entry fixes the existing visit. If opened from a specific visit or editing a review, fixed association; do not silently change it. Global score 0–5 at 0.1 precision, one interactive star strip with tap/drag and live numeric score + reactive emoji (preserve existing thresholds). Zero selectable directly at leading edge or equivalent accessible action; do not show standalone `Dar zero` button. Provide accessible adjustable semantics and keyboard controls; distinguish no selection from explicit zero. Do not add a second visible slider. Partial star rendering follows each star fraction, not a single clip across gaps.

  Use exact existing source descriptions. Session 4C replaces the earlier cards with one horizontal emoji scale per category and a discrete slider aligned to emoji centres. Category name sits left; the current descriptive label sits right and wraps with enlarged text. Emoji positions stay on one line. Selected emoji has a contrasting surface and border; preserve 48dp touch targets, tap/drag, keyboard and descriptive adjustable semantics. No thumb/selected emoji until answered; an explicit minimum remains a real answer. No displayed internal 0–4 or 0–2 scores in these inputs. Preserve required category validation.
- Step 3: title `O que torna este trono especial?` and immediately underneath `As etiquetas são opcionais. Escolhe as que descrevem a tua experiência.` Search, selected removable chips, a bounded set of suggestions (e.g. six); search results shown in bounded/scrollable list, not entire catalogue. ## Session 3 — Collection, rankings, profile and settings

### Collection
- Header `Coleção`, aligned progress summary (unique discovered / current local catalogue total, a ring or bar; no false progress for empty catalogue) and equal-width stat numbers with short labels: visits, reviews, favourites. Use responsive grid, not uneven rectangular chips.
- Tabs `Tronos` and `Diário`, independently scrollable and reachable without scrolling through the entire catalogue. Two-column collectible grid on phones when space permits, adaptive columns on wide screens; discovered vs undiscovered clearly differentiated with text and icon/state. Use simple typography, optional real icon/photo only if genuine metadata exists; do not invent building metadata from names or repeat identical bathroom icon on every card. Keep full name, visit count and navigation. Future catalogue organization remains open; private bathrooms are removed from the vision.
- Diary groups by local calendar date, most recent first, with time/name and rating only if reviewed; no pending labels. Keep direct navigation to bathroom and personal history. All existing visits count correctly.

### Rankings
- Header `Rankings`. Two scope tabs/segmented control `Comunidade` / `Pessoal`. Below, two distinct compact tiles: `Geral` (trophy, golden selected accent) and `Por categoria` (chart, clear selected state). Default to Geral; show no metric tabs in that mode. Por categoria reveals four underlined tabs: `Higiene`, `Conforto`, `Estética`, `Papel`; retain the last category when toggling modes. No category dropdown and no bulky outlined chips. Use short labels, scroll only when required on narrow screens / large text, make selected item visible. A compact separate sort toggle `Melhores` / `Piores` with directional icon; accessible labels and clear current state.
- Rank rows: small plain rank number or subtle top-three medals, name, relevant review count, prominent score with correct denominator or clear scale. Optional thin normalized bar. No large numbered circles; no fake podium/medals for worst rankings. Keep deterministic ranking/tie policy and missing-score exclusion; explicit zero remains valid. Personal ranking uses mean of all own reviews (distinct from latest personal rating in Explore/details). Community = all available local reviews now, future public reviews only. Do not imply other live users exist. Retain detail navigation and all categories.

### Profile and Settings
- Header `Perfil`, settings gear, neutral 60dp person avatar / `O teu perfil`, subtitle `Explorador local`, integrated with the four statistics in one compact card separated by a subtle divider. Real derived stats in aligned grid: discoveries, visits, reviews, favourites. Optional real additional stats only if meaningful (e.g. most visited bathroom); no invented accounts, XP or achievements. Compact navigation rows to favourites and diary (reuse shared views, avoid duplicating full lists on profile). Favourites list uses names and ratings only if meaningful, not repetitive icons. Settings page exposes persisted theme toggle; remove global app bar toggle. Keep privacy/local-data explanation concise and available. No fake authentication.

## A3.1 functional simplification and A4 backlog

Owner decision, 2026-11-27: A3 functional tests are accepted. Its technical product model is superseded by A3.1: one shared catalogue (official/community), anonymous automatic public submission of new reviews, private personal activity, server-authorized bathroom creation, and concise account storage acceptance. Historical private records remain in separate Settings recovery; they are never automatically published, merged or deleted. Public usernames, manual publication and catalogue association are removed from normal flows. Signed-out navigation is Explore / Rankings / Account, with no personal tabs/statistics. Account management, password changes, logout, diagnostics and administrator controls belong in Settings. These are functional corrections now, not approval to start the A4 redesign.

A4 consolidates the earlier Explore + / empty-state feedback and current owner observations:

- **Explore and discovery:** + primarily starts a review; creation is a separate entitled action. Remove catalogue/publication/association jargon. Revisit empty/offline/filtered/new-account states, filter grouping and discoverability, card density, official/community identity and personal versus community scores/counts. Preserve genuine data and distinguish fictional development content.
- **Bathroom details and history:** review summary/actions/tabs, compact shared context, lazy history layout, private visit dates, visit registration/invitation/dismissal and exact safe undo. Keep anonymous browsing free of personal tabs and provide understandable sign-in prompts.
- **Review composition:** reassess global rating control and precision/zero, category layout/captions, tag search/suggestions/custom entry and optional comments. Preserve original humorous descriptions, answer validation, accessibility and exact review/visit associations. Explain anonymous public submission in plain language before saving.
- **Rankings:** improve community/personal selection and global/category hierarchy, selectors, podium/list density, metric denominators and unrated states. Preserve arithmetic, deterministic ties and published-only community scope.
- **Collection and dates:** revisit collection progress/grid, diary/day grouping, visit versus review dates and repeated visits without implying public chronology. Keep favourites/personal history private.
- **Identity, profile and Settings:** simplify headings, icons, grouping and visual hierarchy; identify the private signed-in email clearly. Keep account/security/logout, detailed sync/recovery and team-only tools out of the main experience. Reduce lengthy notices while linking detailed provisional privacy information.
- **Anonymous navigation:** review Explore / Rankings / Account, sign-in entry and account-required actions. Do not reintroduce a writable guest identity or empty personal statistics.

A4 proposals require their own design work and acceptance. A3.1 does not redesign palettes, the review wizard, rankings, collection or the overall visual system. Power-up acquisition/consumption, achievements/XP and A5 are outside this milestone. A development-only idempotent fictional backend and account selector support future design acceptance; neither may enter distributable builds or managed environments.
